HARNESS ENGINEERING: harness changes (agent/skill/command/plugin/config files, cues, wrapper scripts, template/, onboarding) belong to the ai-engineer role and are one-task-one-PR. The reviewer role differs from the implementer; never review your own harness PR and never merge from a worker session. Keep template/ and live copies in sync in the same change (role files, index rows, counts, installer payload). Adding a canonical role needs explicit owner approval.
